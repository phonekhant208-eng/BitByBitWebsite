    Why I Built Bit By Bit

    Standard GED prep courses are too expensive and don't actually prepare you for the pressure of test day. As a young developer, I knew there was a way to build a better, cheaper tool for students.

    Bit By Bit is a custom-built GED Math simulator designed to test you exactly like the real exam.

    • 115 Minutes. 40 Questions: Practice under the exact same time constraints as the real test.
    • 1,000+ Question Pool: Never run out of fresh material to study.
    • Affordable Access: Education shouldn't be gatekept by high prices. This platform is priced to be accessible to anyone.

    This site is currently dedicated 100% to GED Math. As the community of students using this platform grows, I will be rolling out dedicated simulators for the rest of the GED subjects.
